You build the **bosses** for the `dross` Forge mod (Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47.4.10, Java 17, package `com.reg21meme.dross`). The first one is the **Cinder Colossus**, a golem mini-boss that serves the Fire Necromancer.

Read `CLAUDE.md` first and follow its "Rules for every agent". In short:
- Forge 1.20.1 APIs only, `DeferredRegister` for registrations. GeckoLib **4.8.4** for Forge 1.20.1 (package `software.bernie.geckolib`, with `core.animation`, `core.object` etc.; not GeckoLib 5's API).
- Shared files (`Dross.java`, `DrossColors.java`, `registry/*`, `en_us.json`): only ADD your entries, never change or remove anyone else's.
- Colors come from `DrossColors` (add a labeled constant in a "Boss (boss-builder)" section when you need one). The golems serve the Fire Necromancer: **no blue or electric accents** on them; fire colors only (texture colors live in the Python palettes in `tools/golems/kit/materials.py`).
- Stay in your area. If you need another area's work, report it as "Needs from <area>: ..." instead of doing it.
- Run `.\gradlew.bat build --console=plain` after changes and fix errors in your code.
- Explain simply (the user is a beginner). Don't commit.

## How the Cinder Colossus works (built, showcase only)
- **The design: 3G Saddleback** (`GolemDesigns.ALL` holds only it; `tools/golems/designs/d3g_saddleback.py`, parts shared in `designs/calderon.py`): the user's pick from round 3 — two great volcanoes merged into one saddle of rock on its back, big back spikes, 3H's horns, a beard, dark stone. Build the fight on it. The other candidates of rounds 1-3 are archived in `tools/golems/designs/round1/`-`round3/` and `boss-previews/round1/`-`round3/` (not in the game).
- **The user's rules for the look** (CLAUDE.md step 8): volcanoes and spikes grow out of the back at its own angle (never forced upright, never hanging off), feet sunk into it; eruptions (the blast, the lava form's fountain, the particles) shoot out along each volcano, never straight up; dark stone; lava always in the crater; spikes up off the shoulders; horns; the beard; a deep forward lean before the eruption; smooth limbs (no snapping). `tools/golems/kit/clipping.py` checks attachment and pass-through on every build: keep its report clean (a light forearm-into-side touch during the lava form's walk is known). The shoulder pads hang on `pauldron_mount_*` bones that follow only part of the arm's swing: every animation must call `Rig.follow(pose)` on its frames (tools/golems/kit/anims.py), and a hand-made animation must turn the mounts back too, or the pads slide through the chest again.
- **Three stages** (`GolemPhase`): `DORMANT` (the rock shell, `shell_*` bones, dim cracks, the lava's glow turned down to `GolemDesigns.DORMANT_GLOW` and brightening as it erupts, the volcano smoking), `ERUPTED` (the volcano has blown and lava has poured over the shell: the flow layer), `CORE` (the shell gone: the lava body, a bigger lava volcano `molten_*` and its live eruption `vent_*`).
- **Synced state** on `CinderColossus`: design number, phase, `GolemAction` (`IDLE`, `WALK`, `ERUPT`, `BREAK_SHELL`, `DEATH`, `STATUE`) and an action counter (a repeated action restarts its animation). Two GeckoLib controllers: `main` (the body, 6-tick blends: `animation.cinder_colossus.<idle|walk|erupt|break_shell|death|statue>`) and `volcano` (the volcano's own bones: `volcano_burst` while erupting, `volcano_erupting` in the core form). `ERUPT` ends in `ERUPTED`, `BREAK_SHELL` in `CORE`, `DEATH` in `STATUE` (server side, by `GolemDesigns` timings). Both controllers blend into a new animation over the same `ANIMATION_BLEND_TICKS` (6) and GeckoLib only starts the animation after that, so anything timed to an animation (particles, the lava pour, the fades) uses `animationSeconds()`, not `secondsInAction()`; keep the two controllers' blends equal so the body and its volcanoes stay in step.
- **Rendering**: four passes — the main texture (world-lit); `GolemFlowLayer` (the `_flow` texture drawn with Minecraft's dissolve shader, `RenderType.dragonExplosionAlpha`, the one the ender dragon's death uses: a pixel drops out while its alpha, 1 - 0.25 x how far the lava has to travel (at most 254), is below the vertex alpha, so lowering the vertex alpha from 1 to 0.75 over `FLOW_START`-`FLOW_END` of the eruption pours the lava down from the crater; the crater pools are alpha 255, so at progress 0 they're all that shows and the craters always hold bright lava; while dying it's drawn as an emissive layer that fades out). Vanilla entity shaders test the *texture* alpha before the vertex colour, which is why the dissolve shader is used; `GolemGlowLayer` (the `_glow` texture full-bright); `GolemStatueLayer` (the `_statue` obsidian texture faded in during death). The glow and flow passes bend normals halfway to "up" so lava stays bright on the sides. GeckoLib's own `AutoGlowingGeoLayer` is deliberately not used (it cuts holes in the base texture, which breaks the fades). `burst_*` bones only show while erupting. The volcanoes lean with the back, and their fountain, bombs and blast follow them (frame bones `*_spout`, `*_blast`); the renderer also tracks each `*_crater` bone's `*_crater_aim` bone, so the golem's particles (`CinderColossus.Crater`: where, and which way) shoot out along each volcano too (lava pops, which ignore their speed, are scattered along the jet).
- **Particles** (client, `CinderColossus.spawnParticles`): the renderer tracks the `*_crater` bones and tells the golem where they are; dormant: a lazy plume of smoke; the eruption: an explosion, lava, flames and smoke from the crater; erupted: smoke and the odd spark; core: a constant eruption; the shell bursting: an explosion; dying: steam.
- **Death**: the death animation (9.7 s: a 3.4-second scream in two waves, then the collapse) holds its last pose; the glow fades out 4.0–7.2 s and the statue fades in 6.0–9.6 s (`GolemDesigns.GLOW_FADE_*`/`STATUE_FADE_*`); afterwards the action is `STATUE`. The user wants the long scream and the slow cooling (the eruption's and shell-breaking's roars stay short). A real boss death will need `tickDeath` overridden so the body stays (vanilla removes a dead mob after 20 ticks), then either keep the statue entity or swap it for blocks.
- **Showcase golems** (`spawnShowcase`, saved `Showcase`): no AI, silent, can't be pushed, never despawn (not even on Peaceful), only `/kill` hurts them. Right-click moves one on a stage (dormant > erupts > breaks out > dormant again), sneak + right-click dies.
- The attribute numbers in `createAttributes` are placeholders until the fight is designed.

## Changing a design
Edit the Python design (`tools/golems/designs/d3*_*.py`, shared parts in `designs/calderon.py`), then `python tools/golems/build.py` (all designs, so `GolemDesigns.java` and `all_golems.png` stay complete), then the Gradle build. Look at the previews (`boss-previews/NN_<id>_sheet.png`) before testing in game. The previews use GeckoLib's exact maths, so they match the game. Animations are generated (`tools/golems/kit/anims.py`): the walk loop moves the golem `Design.walkBlocks()` blocks per `walkTicks()` — scale the walk animation's speed by the real movement speed so the knuckles don't slide.
