# Fire candidates (tools/fire)

Makes the frozen candidate models of the Cinder Wastes, in three sets (`SETS` in `build.py`), each with its own
gallery in the game:

- **`servants`** (files `firedesigns/fNN_*.py`, labels "1A"...): the candidate looks for the Fire Necromancer's two
  open servant places (the Cinder Colossus is the first; the boss-site planner keeps Cinder Wastes slots 1 and 2 free,
  CLAUDE.md step 12). The user asked (October 26, 2026) for **fifteen bosses in a line, each with three or four
  variations behind it, models only**, mixed sizes, and left the creative decisions to Claude.
  `/dross showcase boss_designs cinder_wastes`.
- **`emberdactyl`** (`eNN_*.py`, labels "E1A"...): the user's Rodan-like volcanic pterosaur, the **Emberdactyl**
  (October 26: "5-6 candidate designs of the adult, each with a matching young reborn version (smaller, sleeker,
  brighter), plus the egg and its nest ... make sure that good looking"): six adults (`firekit/ptero.py` builds them
  all, each design module a set of choices), each one's young behind it, and three eggs.
  `/dross showcase boss_designs emberdactyl` builds them and its volcano behind them (Java: `boss/emberdactyl/`).
- **`cinder_mobs`** (`mNN_*.py`, labels "M1A"...): the Cinder Wastes' regular mobs, the user's picks of the servant
  candidates: the Pallbearer (11A, his first look, at his full 8.7 blocks) and the Cinder Hermit in ten shells.
  `/dross showcase cinder_mobs`.

A set's designs are numbered after the sets before it (the servants 1-60), so every number stays the same when a set
grows; `ONE_ROW = True` in a design module puts each of its looks in a column of its own (the hermit's shells).

Everything is generated by Python, never drawn by hand, the same way as the Rimefall candidates (`tools/rimefall`),
whose kit this reuses (`rimekit`: shapes, per-face UVs, the floating-piece and z-fighting checks, the previews); see
`BOSS_GUIDE.md`.

## Running it

From the project folder:

```
python tools/fire/build.py                    # everything, every set (about 5-15 minutes)
python tools/fire/build.py --set emberdactyl  # one set: its files and previews (the Java list needs a full build)
python tools/fire/build.py --only 1 E2 M2     # only these designs (every look): files and previews
python tools/fire/build.py --quick <dir> 1 E2 # quick previews of these designs into <dir> (nothing in the project)
python tools/fire/build.py --board <dir> E1   # one picture per design: every look from the front, three-quarter,
                                              # behind and at night (for checking a design at a glance)
python tools/fire/build.py --previews-only    # previews only; the game files are left alone
python tools/fire/zcheck.py 11A               # lists any face pairs that would still flicker
python tools/fire/volcano_preview.py          # the Emberdactyl's volcano previews (after its probe, below)
python tools/fire/mobs.py                     # the designs that move: their skeletons, animations, previews
python tools/fire/mobs.py hermit --no-gif     # one mob, without the GIFs (much quicker)
python tools/fire/sounds.py                   # their sounds (after mobs.py: the moments come from the animations)
```

**The designs that move** (`mobs.py`, the modules in `firemobs/`; it reuses the Rimefall animals' kit,
`tools/rimefall/rimekit/mobkit.py`, and the golem kit's IK): the Emberdactyl (`emberdactyl`, E1A), its young
(`emberyoung`, E2A: the adult's moves, 1.6 times as fast), the Pallbearer (`pallbearer`, M1A) and the Cinder Hermit
(`hermit`, M2A-M2J: a model per shell, one set of animations). Each design's parts are routed to bones by the design
itself (`s.part(name)`; a statue puts them all on its one bone), so `build.py` and `mobs.py` build the same look. It
writes `geo/entity/firemob/<id>.geo.json`, `textures/entity/firemob/<id>.png` (and its `_glow`, `_clear`,
`_clearglow`), `animations/entity/firemob/<mob>.animation.json`, the generated `boss/fire/FireAnims.java` (each mob's
animations, kept in `firemobs/anims.json` so one mob can be built alone) and the previews in
`boss-previews/cinder_wastes/<set>/animations/`. Every frame is checked: nothing under the floor, the head and wings
(claws, eyes) not through each other. `firemobs.ANIMATED` says which gallery designs wear which mob (FireDesigns'
`mob`, written by `build.py`).

**Their sounds** (`sounds.py`, the Rat King's synthesizer and the Huntsman's `voicekit.py`, all original):
`assets/dross/sounds/emberdactyl/`, `emberyoung/`, `pallbearer/`, `hermit/`, their `sounds.json` events and subtitles,
the generated `boss/fire/FireSounds.java` (every event, and `CUES`: which sound each animation makes when), and the page
`boss-previews/cinder_wastes/sounds/index.html`. The Emberdactyl's are kaiju-sized (the user, October 27: "deeper ... a
kaiju-level noise"): its cries (`kaiju_cry`) are a Rodan-like trilling, metallic shriek sung by five voices a little out
of tune, over a roaring throat two and three octaves down and a sub-bass, echoing across a valley (`valley`); its young's
are its first three (the user kept them for the young; the grown one's own are about half their pitch over a heavier
growl, `ed_screech_deep`). Every sound with several versions is also one event per version, `<event>.v<n>`: the
showcase models play them in turn, or always the version its event's `pick` says (the Pallbearer's roar and death: 1,
until the user picks). An animation `anims.json` doesn't have yet just has its sounds left out (a note says so).
`python tools/fire/sounds.py --only emberdactyl,pallbearer/roar` makes only those folders' (or those) sounds; a sound
whose samples haven't changed is never written again (its fingerprint is in the Ogg's comment).

The volcano is built by the game, not here; its previews are drawn from the blocks the game really placed. First the
probe (it builds the volcano on a flat plain in a test world and writes what you could see to
`run/probe/ember_volcano.txt`), then the drawing:

```
.\gradlew.bat runGameTestServer -PgameTestNamespaces=dross_volcano -PgameTestWorld=<a new folder> --console=plain
python tools/fire/volcano_preview.py
```

Needs Python 3 with numpy and Pillow.

## What it writes

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `assets/dross/geo/entity/fire/<id>.geo.json` | the model (per-face UVs) |
| `assets/dross/textures/entity/fire/<id>.png` | the solid parts (flames are cut out, like Minecraft's fire block) |
| `.../<id>_glow.png` | what glows: lava, embers, eyes, flames |
| `.../<id>_clear.png` | what you see through: glass, heat haze |
| `.../<id>_clearglow.png` | what glows on the see-through parts |
| `assets/dross/animations/entity/fire/candidate.animation.json` | empty: nothing moves |
| `boss/fire/FireDesigns.java` | the list the game reads (generated: the three sets' lists, labels, names, measured sizes, which column and row) |
| `boss-previews/cinder_wastes/<folder>/<label>_<id>.png` | one look from four sides, at night and from above (`<folder>`: `servants`, `emberdactyl`, `mobs`) |
| `boss-previews/cinder_wastes/<folder>/originals.png` | each design's first look |
| `boss-previews/cinder_wastes/<folder>/all_designs_<n>.png` | every look, a row per design, five designs a page |
| `boss-previews/cinder_wastes/<folder>/lineup.png` | every look at its real size, a row per design, next to a player |
| `boss-previews/cinder_wastes/<folder>/index.html` | all of it on one page (the Emberdactyl's with its volcano) |
| `boss-previews/cinder_wastes/emberdactyl/volcano_*.png` | the volcano (`volcano_preview.py`): front, back, night, from above, the crater |

The mod draws the four textures in that order (`boss/fire/client/FireCandidateRenderer`): solid, glow, clear, clear
glow; the glow comes before the clear parts so a flame in a lantern or lava in a glass belly shines through the glass.

## How it's built

- `firekit/fmats.py`: the fire materials (lava orange and obsidian, the Fire Necromancer's colours; never the Dross
  blue): `lava` (streaks running downhill, optional cooled crust), `crust` (dark plates, glowing cracks), `obsidian`
  (glassy facets, optional lava veins), `charred` (charred wood's blocky checks, embers in some cracks), `ash`,
  `flame` (cut out by default, like Minecraft's fire block), `heat` (the Heat Haze's shimmer), `brick` (firebrick
  bond), `clay`, `bronze`/brass with patina, `wicker`, `star_iron` (the Starborn's dark metal, white-hot seams),
  `burnt_cloth` (a hem eaten away, its edge smouldering), `ember_feathers`, `chitin_glow`, `beetle` (a beetle's
  green-to-violet sheen following the curve of its shell), `sulfur`, `wings` (a moth's: veins, bands, glowing
  eyespots), `smoke`; and the Rimefall kit's own materials with their frost turned off.
- `firekit/parts.py`: `Xform` (a part built upright and then turned and moved into place: a hunched torso, a raised
  head, a toppled bell), `leg` (a jointed limb with knuckles of their own material), `flames` (tongues of crossed
  plates like Minecraft's fire, each with its own small offset so no two plates share a plane).
- `firekit/ptero.py`: the Emberdactyl (all six adults and their young): a raised V of wings (a membrane of plates
  between the finger bones, its trailing edge ragged, a glowing knuckle at each wrist), a beaked head with a glowing
  seam along its jaw and one of five crests, a body with its legs and talons, a short or long tail; the young built
  from the same choices at 0.56 of the size, a bigger head, slimmer, its hide hotter.
- `firedesigns/fNN_<id>.py` (and `eNN_`, `mNN_`): one module per design: `NUMBER`, `ID` (no underscores), `NAME` (at most 15 characters: it
  goes on a sign), `BLURB`, `VARIANTS` (each a dict with its `letter`, its `mood` (the sign's third line, at most 15
  characters), a `note`, and whatever the design reads), `FREE` (materials meant to float: smoke, flung debris),
  `build(s, v)` and `materials(v)`. A look's id is the boss's id plus its letter (`widowa`); the first look (A) is the
  original, the others its variations.
