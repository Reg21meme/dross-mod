# Round 2 of the elemental knights (archived)

The candidates of November 9, 2026 that the user didn't keep, and the round 2 versions of the two designs that changed
after it (`f01_cinder.py` with its flail and twin blades, `t03_wreck.py` with all three anchor knights). Their previews
are in `boss-previews/knights/round2/`, the whole of round 2 as it was. These modules aren't built any more (the build
only reads `knightdesigns/` itself); any of them can be brought back by copying it up a folder.

The user's picks and notes (November 9):
- **Thunder:** "T3A is the best": the Wreck Knight (the diver with his anchor on his shoulder). T3B Stormwake and T3C
  Stormcaptain dropped. His partner: F1C's twin blades given to a lightning knight, "like a speed ninja with lightning
  attacks" (T6 Stormblade, round 3), so T1 Storm Knight, T2 Tidecaller, T4 Thunder Warden and T5 Storm Sentinel are here.
- **Fire:** "make the 2nd fire dude the fire hammer": F1B's burning flail became a fire hammer (round 3); F1C's twin
  blades went to the thunder ninja. F1A's greataxe and F5 Flamehand stay.
- **Ice:** "make the ice dudes the same heights as everyone else": the Thawed Soldiers (9C, 9D) now 4.6 blocks to their
  horns' tips (were 4), their heads where the knights' are.
