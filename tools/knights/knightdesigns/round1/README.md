# Round 1 of the elemental knights (archived)

The fifteen candidates of November 7, 2026, as they were when the user looked at them in the game. Their previews are in
`boss-previews/knights/round1/`. These modules aren't built any more (the build only reads `knightdesigns/` itself); they
are kept as a record, and any of them can be brought back by copying it up a folder.

The user's picks and notes (November 7):
- **Poison:** P1 Blight Warden and P3 Sporebound (P2 Plague Herald dropped).
- **Earth:** E3 Geode Knight and E4 Rockfall Knight (E1 Graven Knight and E2 Crag Bulwark dropped).
- **Fire:** F1 Cinder Knight's look kept, with other weapons (round 2: three options), and a new knight with only fire
  hands as his partner. F2 Pyre Templar, F3 Ember Lancer and F4 Furnace Knight dropped.
- **Thunder:** "why is your thunder idea just copper ... it needs to be blue to reflect the Dross". Round 2 is all in
  the Dross's electric blue. T3 Wreck Knight gets three new designs; later in his fight he throws his anchor at you and
  pulls it back on its chain. A new standard thunder knight is his partner. T1, T2 and T4 come back in blue as more
  options. The copper ones are here.
