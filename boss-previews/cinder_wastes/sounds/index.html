The fire mobs' sounds

All original: made from scratch by tools/fire/sounds.py. Each player is one version, numbered; the showcase models play a sound's versions in turn, or the one marked *. The Emberdactyl's screech is in the spirit of a giant film pterosaur's cry (piercing, trilling, metallic, echoing across a valley) with a kaiju's roaring throat and weight under it, made from nothing. In the game: /dross showcase boss_sounds emberdactyl and /dross showcase mob_sounds cinder_wastes.
