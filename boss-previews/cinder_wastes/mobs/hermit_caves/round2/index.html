The hermit crab caves, round 2 (archived: the user kept Nos. 2, 4 and 5; No. 2 then made bigger, the current page is here)

No. 7's style (a Cinder Vent den on a mound of basalt and magma, burrows off it, vents and walled-in lava), one Cinder Hermit each. Hillside dens are built into a hill: the way in is in the hillside's face, the hill is the roof; the cave is walled in by its own shell of rock, and where the hill is lower than the shell, the shell shows as a rocky outcrop (the second picture: a hill half as high). Underground dens lie on flat land, their floor 3 blocks down, their roof a low mound of ash over them, with a loot room behind a crawl the crab can't follow. No stairs or slabs anywhere: steps are whole blocks. Every lava block is walled in under and on all four sides, every floor cell can be walked to from the way in (at the south) and is lit. In the game: /dross showcase hermit_caves; in the world, 1-2 in every Cinder Wastes area, hillside ones where the land rises (/dross goto hermit_cave). Round 1's seven: round1/.

1. Hillside Burrows (hillside)

No. 7 dug into a hill: a rocky mouth in the hillside, a round den round a Cinder Vent on its mound of basalt and magma, and three burrows off it: the crab's nest at the back, a pool of lava, and a midden of its spare shells.

One crab spot, 6 lava blocks, its darkest floor cell lit 5.

2. Long Gallery (hillside)

A wide mouth in the hillside onto a long gallery dug along the hill, two pillars holding its roof and two Cinder Vents on their mounds; a pool of lava at its west end, a midden of spare shells at its east end, and the crab's nest in a burrow back into the hill.

One crab spot, 4 lava blocks, its darkest floor cell lit 4.

3. Claw Burrows (hillside)

A narrow mouth in the hillside, a step down into a round den round its Cinder Vent, and three burrows fanned out behind it like a claw's fingers: a pool of lava, the crab's nest deepest in the hill, and a midden of its spare shells.

One crab spot, 5 lava blocks, its darkest floor cell lit 5.

4. Ash Mound Den (underground)

A cutting slopes down a block every two into a den 3 blocks under the ground round its Cinder Vent, its roof a low mound of ash with a second vent smoking on top; off it the crab's nest, a pool of lava, and behind a crawl the crab can't follow, a little loot room of decorated pots, suspicious gravel and a barrel.

One crab spot, 3 lava blocks, its darkest floor cell lit 4, loot: 1 barrel, 4 pot, 2 dig.

5. Twin Hump Den (underground)

Two humps of ash over a shallow den: a cutting slopes down to a vent chamber 3 blocks under the ground, the crab's nest under the bigger hump, a pool of lava to the north, and through a crawl the crab can't follow, a loot room of decorated pots, suspicious gravel and a barrel.

One crab spot, 3 lava blocks, its darkest floor cell lit 5, loot: 1 barrel, 3 pot, 2 dig.
