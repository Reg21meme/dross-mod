The hunter villages: the Sunken Warren

Your pick: Sunken Hollow and The Warren combined, now built from your hand-designed igloos (step 2 of the plan of October 20). Courtyards dug into the snow, each ringed by sunken igloos, joined by sunken lanes; more igloos behind the rings, joined by covered snow passages. Nine layouts of 15 to 20 igloos (the shrine, standing alone, in 3, 6 and 9), and two Huntsman's villages of 26 and 29 (his lodge, his yard and the Alpha Moose's barn). A ring of lights round each. Every decoration is a 1 in 2 chance when the game places a village (the second picture of each shows one roll). In the game: /dross showcase hunter_villages. Round 1's ten designs are in round1/.
