The elemental knights' sounds

All original: made from scratch by tools/knights/sounds.py and its voice engine knightvoice.py. Each element has its own character, each knight his own weight: the heavy ones low (much of their sound under 200 Hz), the light ones high and short. Their voices are no one's voice: no throat, no vowels, each made of his element inside his sealed helm. /dross showcase boss_sounds knights plays them in the game.

No one's voice: how voice-like, against the Rimefall folk

Idle, grunts, hurts and death of each (tools/knights/voicecheck.py). periodicity: how strongly the sound repeats at a pitch a voice could have (white noise 0.11, a buzz 1.0); harmonic: how far one comb of harmonics stands out of its gaps (noise about 4 dB); vowel band: its share of energy at 250-2500 Hz, where vowels live. The Huntsman and the Frostfolk are voices (made with the Huntsman's voicekit); the knights must not be.
