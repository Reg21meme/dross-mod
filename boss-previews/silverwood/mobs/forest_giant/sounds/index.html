The Silverwood Giant's sounds

All original: made from scratch by tools/forest_giant/sounds.py. New (November 13, 2026): its voice remade way deeper and monstrous (the user: "their voices need to be way deeper and monstrous and have a presence"): its pitch about 26-56 Hz (a man's is about 100), a mouth and throat twice a man's size, two throats a little out of tune with each other, a growl an octave under it, gravel in its throat, the weight of its chest under it all, and the forest's hills throwing it back (echoes and a long tail); its voice sounds carry further (their fade-out distance in brackets, blocks; Minecraft's default is 16). Every voice sound has three versions to pick from (the tear, the sweep and the smash two). There's no separate fall sound any more: it dies the vanilla way, and each death version has its body crashing down in it about 0.65 s in. The stamp, the steps, the stomp and the boulder's crash are as they were.

Each player is one version; in the game an event plays one of its versions at random. Each version is also its own event (its id under it), so you can pick: tell me the numbers you like ("roar 2, death 1 and 3").
